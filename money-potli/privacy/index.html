1. Who we are

Money Potli (package io.github.mrasikv.moneypotli) is a personal finance app developed by Mohammed Rasi KV, an independent developer ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what the app does with your information on Android and on the web version.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage (on the web version, in your browser's storage). This includes transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, loans, investments, people you lend to or borrow from, bill photos and your settings.

There is no Money Potli account, no sign-up and no developer server. The developer cannot see, access or recover your data.

3. Permissions and features

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no, and the rest of the app keeps working.

SMS (read bank messages) — Android only

  • Used only when you choose to import bank messages, for the date range you pick.
  • Messages are read and understood on your phone to find the amount, account and shop. Only the entries you confirm are saved in the app.
  • SMS content is never uploaded or sent anywhere. One-time passwords (OTPs) are skipped.
  • You can also paste a message instead of granting the permission.

Microphone (voice entry)

  • Used only while you use voice entry to speak an entry, such as "spent 250 on groceries".
  • Your speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service. On most Android phones this is provided by Google and may process the audio online under Google's Privacy Policy. Money Potli receives only the text and does not record or store audio.

Camera and photos (receipt scanning)

  • When you scan a bill, the system camera or photo picker opens and you choose the picture. The app does not take photos on its own.
  • Text on the bill is read on your phone (on-device text recognition). The photo is not uploaded for this.
  • A compressed copy of the photo is saved with the entry on your device, so you can see it later.

Contacts (choosing a person)

When you add a friend from your contacts, the system contact picker opens and only the one contact you choose (name and number) is saved. The app does not read your contact list and has no contacts permission.

Fingerprint / face unlock

Biometric unlock is handled by your phone's system. The app is told only whether unlock succeeded; it never receives your fingerprint or face data.

Notifications

Reminders for card bills, EMIs and budgets are created and shown on your device. While an app lock is set, amounts are left out of notifications.

4. What goes over the internet

The app connects to the internet only for the following. All connections use HTTPS. None of your personal or financial data is sent — only the codes needed to look up a price.

Currency exchange ratesopen.er-api.com, and a public currency rate file via cdn.jsdelivr.netNothing personal; the app downloads the full rate list
Mutual fund prices (optional)api.mfapi.inThe fund's scheme code
Crypto prices (optional)api.coingecko.comThe coin's id
Stock prices (optional)query1.finance.yahoo.comThe stock symbol
Gold and silver prices (optional)api.gold-api.comThe metal
Backup (optional)Google DriveYour backup file, to your own Drive (see below)

Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the app connects. Rates and prices are saved on your device so the app also works offline.

5. Google Drive backup (optional)

  • Off unless you turn it on and sign in with your Google account.
  • Backups are saved to your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder, using Google's limited drive.appdata access. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The developer has no access to your Drive or your backups.
  • Your use of Google sign-in and Drive is also covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing you start

  • Reports and reminders: when you share a balance, statement or reminder (for example to WhatsApp), the app hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you do it.
  • Sync with a friend: sync files are encrypted (AES-256) with a code only the two of you know, and travel through whichever app you choose to send them.
  • View on computer: an optional, read-only view served directly from your phone to a browser on your own Wi-Fi or hotspot, protected by a one-time code. It does not go through the internet or any server.
  • Exports: CSV and backup files are saved where you choose.

7. What we never do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • The developer receives no data from the app.

8. Optional tips

The app may offer optional tips to support the developer, paid through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. As with any Play purchase, Google provides the developer with a basic order record (such as the order number, the tip chosen and the country), never your card details or anything from inside the app. Tips are entirely optional and unlock nothing.

9. How your data is protected

  • Optional PIN and biometric lock; the PIN is stored only as a secure salted hash.
  • Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked while the lock or privacy mode is on.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device copy are turned off for the app's data, so it only leaves the phone through backups you control.

Your data is as safe as your device. Use a screen lock on your phone, and keep exported backup files somewhere private.

10. Your control and deletion

  • Delete in the app: erase all data from the app's settings.
  • Uninstall: removes all app data from the phone.
  • Drive backups: the app keeps only the last 5. To delete them, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Money Potli → Delete hidden app data.
  • Web version: clear the site's data in your browser.

Because the developer does not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

11. Children

Money Potli is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
