The business side

What changes for a centre

Every module started from a real problem at the counter, then became a rule the system enforces for you.

The problem

A busy counter, paper tokens and retyped details

What the centre gets

A front desk built for busy counters: a token queue, ready-made service bundles and the customer confirming details and price before work starts.

Why it works

Details come from the customer, not retyped, so the counter moves faster with fewer mistakes.

The problem

Government fees mixed with revenue and VAT

What the centre gets

Every service has a service charge and a government fee. Invoices show both, charge 5% VAT only on the service charge, and feed the VAT 201 return and e-invoicing.

Why it works

Prices and tax are calculated on the server, never in the browser, so no screen can produce a wrong invoice.

The problem

Visa files lost between steps and WhatsApp chats

What the centre gets

Each applicant has one visa file that tracks every step, deadline and payment, with a status link the customer can open.

Why it works

Customers are kept informed automatically, so staff spend less time answering "what's the status?".

The problem

Expired documents noticed too late

What the centre gets

A compliance hub that lists every expiring passport, visa, Emirates ID and trade licence, sends reminders, and turns them into follow-ups and renewal jobs.

Why it works

Expiries become repeat business instead of lost customers.

The problem

Cash gaps and changes nobody can explain

What the centre gets

Cash drawer shifts with counted totals, company cards reconciled against government fees, discounts and voids that need a manager's approval, and a full audit trail.

Why it works

Money records can't be quietly edited; every correction leaves a trace the owner can see.

The problem

Attendance, leave and salaries in spreadsheets

What the centre gets

Staff check-in, leave with approvals, and payroll with gratuity and the WPS salary file, with warnings when a decision goes against UAE labour law.

Why it works

UAE labour rules are built in, and staff data is handled with consent under UAE data-protection law.

The engineering side

Built like a bank, not a spreadsheet

One platform serves many centres. The architecture was designed from day one for security, correct money and growth.

Security

Each centre's data stays its own

Isolation between centres is enforced by the database itself, not just the app, and every action is checked against the person's role and branch.

Benefit: safe to run many centres on one platform.

Correctness

Money that always adds up

Prices, VAT and balances are calculated on the server and kept as a permanent record, so cash, invoices and reports always agree.

Benefit: accounts an auditor can trust.

Reliability

Keeps working on bad connections

Everyday work continues offline and syncs safely when the connection returns, without duplicates or lost changes.

Benefit: no lost or double work.

Growth

Ready for more centres and countries

Modules are switched on per centre, country rules are kept separate from the core, and capacity is measured before it's needed.

Benefit: predictable cost as the business grows.
